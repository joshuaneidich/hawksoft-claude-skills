# The HawkSoft Action Menu (navigation)

Every Action-menu task passes through here. On the client toolbar, open **Action**,
then choose one of two modes.

## External vs. Internal

- **External action** — a contact happened. Choose the **Starting Method**, then the
  **Direction**, then the **Party**, then the **Action**.
- **Internal action** — an operation with no contact. Choose **Internal**, then the
  **Action** directly.

## Starting Method (External)

`Phone`, `Mail`, `Walkin`, `Chat`, `Fax`, `Email`, `Text`, `Online`, `Download` —
pick the one that matches how the interaction happened.

## Direction (External)

- **From** — the communication came *from* the other party *to* the agency (inbound).
- **To** — the agency reached out *to* the other party (outbound).

## Party (External)

Who the interaction is with — pick the one that matches; if it is unclear, **stop and
ask** rather than assuming Insured:

- **Insured** — the insured or an authorized insured contact.
- **Carrier** — the insurance carrier.
- **Lien Holder** — a lienholder on a financed asset.
- **Escrow** — an escrow / closing agent.
- **Claimant** — a third-party claimant.
- **Law Enforcement** — police, DMV, or similar.
- **Certificate Holder** — a holder of a certificate of insurance.
- **Mortgagee** — a mortgage company on a property policy.
- **Other** — anyone not listed; note who in the log.

## Action

After the party (External) or under **Internal**, choose the operation. Each has its
own task file:

Change Client/Policy · Change Policy Status · Claims · Log · Trust Accounting ·
Quote/Export · Scan · Print/Create · Inquiry/Website · Send Email · ACORD Forms ·
Insert Policy · Duplicate/Remarket Policy · Archive Policy · Move Policy ·
Duplicate Client · Merge Clients · Archive Client · Create Snapshot.

## Return to the task

Once you have reached the action, return to the task's steps and continue.
